Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Tivon is built so that the most sensitive part — which apps you set up — never leaves your device.

The short version. Your pause history stays on your iPhone. Analytics and crash reporting share one switch; while it is on, Tivon sends an event for each pause, never naming the app. If your App Store region is in the EU / EEA, the UK or Switzerland, it is off until you agree; elsewhere it is on, Tivon tells you so when you first open it, and you can turn it off right there. Either way you can turn it off at any time. We never send the names of the apps you set up. We don’t track you, don’t use advertising identifiers, and don’t sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Which apps you’ve set up
  • Your pause history: which app each pause was for, when it happened, whether you left or continued, and any answer you gave
  • Your settings and preferences

This history is not uploaded to us, and deleting the app removes it. While analytics is on, Tivon also sends a separate event for each pause (see below). It never names the app the pause was for; if that app is in Tivon’s built-in list, it says which category the app is in, such as social or video.

Analytics and crash reports

One switch, in Settings → Analytics & crash reports, controls both. Whether it starts on or off depends on the region of your App Store account, which Tivon checks once, the first time you open it:

  • EU / EEA, the UK and Switzerland: off. When you first open Tivon, it asks. Nothing is sent unless you agree.
  • Everywhere else: on. When you first open Tivon, it shows what is collected, with the switch on, and you can turn it off right there. Nothing is sent before you have seen that screen.
  • If Tivon cannot tell your App Store region, it uses your device’s region; if it cannot tell that either, it treats you as in the first group.

Tivon keeps the result of that first check and does not change it later, and once you have set the switch yourself, your choice always wins. Tivon stores only which of the two groups applies, not your country, and does not send it.

Product analytics — helps us see which parts of the app work. When on, we send events about how Tivon is used, for example that you completed setup, that a pause appeared and how you responded to it, or that you viewed the subscription page or started a subscription. Depending on the event, it can include:

  • For a pause: whether you left or continued, how long you took to choose, the time of day (one of five periods, such as morning), and the pause style shown
  • For setup and automations: how many apps you have set up and how far you got in the setup guide; for an app in Tivon’s built-in list, its category (such as social or video). Never the app’s name or anything you typed
  • For purchases: the Tivon subscription product (monthly or annual), never prices or payment details

Every event also carries your app version and build, iOS version, device model and type, the language Tivon is shown in, your plan (Free or Pro), the calendar week (for example 2026-W38), the exact time of the event, a session ID, random numbers that tie related events together (for example, one per pause), and a randomly generated anonymous identifier, plus technical details such as the version of the consent or notice screen you saw and the version of the analytics library. Events never contain the name or identifier of any app you set up, or the text you entered for it in Shortcuts.

Crash reports — helps us fix crashes. When on, a crash sends the crash type, the error message, the stack of each thread, the list of loaded binaries, and basic app and device information. It also sends how often the app is opened and for how long, with a random installation ID used to count them, which is what makes it possible to calculate a crash rate. Screenshots, view hierarchies and interaction trails are switched off.

The two random identifiers

Product analytics uses a randomly generated anonymous identifier, and crash reporting uses a separate random installation ID. Both are created on your device, contain no personal information, and are not linked to your name, email or Apple Account. Both are deleted from your device when you switch analytics off.

What we never collect

  • The names or identifiers of the apps you set up
  • Your name, email address or contact details (if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write)
  • Location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing or search history
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendor (IDFV), or any cross-app or cross-site tracking

Turning it off

Open Tivon’s settings and switch off Analytics & crash reports. Both services stop immediately and anything still queued on your device is deleted. Nothing that happens while it is off is sent later. Data that has already begun uploading cannot be recalled. The moment you switch it off, Tivon also sends one last event: besides the basic details every event carries (see above), it records only how many days analytics had been on. Nothing more is sent after that.

Who processes the data

Analytics is processed by PostHog, Inc. and crash reports by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), both on servers in the European Union, as processors acting on our instructions. We don’t want either service to keep your IP address: the app asks PostHog not to look up a location from it and sets Sentry not to record it, and both projects are set to discard IP addresses. As with any internet request, the connection itself still reaches their servers from your IP address.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. If product analytics is on, we also receive the purchase events described above, which include the subscription product (monthly or annual). Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app under your Apple Account.

Age

Tivon is made for adults and is not directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone below the age of digital consent where they live — 16 in parts of the EU, 13 in many other places. Where analytics starts on, the first screen says so and it can be switched off right there; switching it off stops collection and deletes whatever is still on the device.

Your rights

Because the identifiers described above are random, are not linked to your name, email or account, and are never shown to you in Tivon, we usually cannot locate “your” data on request. You can stop all collection at any time with the switch above, and delete everything held locally by removing the app. For any question about this policy, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, we will update this page and the date at the top, and ask for consent again where required.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 22 日

Tivon 的设计原则是：最敏感的那部分数据——你设置了哪些 App——永远不离开你的设备。

一句话版本。你的停顿记录只存在你的 iPhone 上。产品分析和崩溃上报共用一个开关；开关开着时，每次停顿会发送一个事件，但从不包含 App 名称。如果你的 App Store 地区在欧盟 / 欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，它默认关闭，你同意之后才开始；在其他地区它默认开启，你第一次打开 Tivon 时会看到说明，可以当场关掉。无论在哪里，都可以随时关闭。我们不会上传你设置的 App 名称，不追踪你，不使用广告标识符，也不出售数据。

只存在你设备上的内容

  • 你设置了哪些 App
  • 你的停顿记录：每次停顿对应的 App、发生的时间、你选择了离开还是继续，以及你给出的回答
  • 你的设置和偏好

这些记录不会上传给我们，删除 App 即一并删除。产品分析开着时，Tivon 还会为每次停顿另外发送一个事件（见下文）。其中从不包含这次停顿对应的 App 名称；如果那个 App 在 Tivon 内置的 App 目录里，会带上它的类别，例如社交或视频。

产品分析与崩溃上报

两者由同一个开关控制，位置在 设置 → 分析与崩溃报告。它默认开还是关，取决于你的 App Store 账户所在地区；Tivon 只在你第一次打开时判断一次：

  • 欧盟 / 欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士：默认关闭。第一次打开 Tivon 时会征求你的同意，你不同意就不发送任何数据。
  • 其他地区：默认开启。第一次打开 Tivon 时会说明收集哪些内容，开关是开着的，你可以当场关掉。看到这一页之前不会发送任何数据。
  • 如果 Tivon 判断不出你的 App Store 地区，就改看设备的地区设置；两者都判断不出时，按第一种处理。

第一次判断的结果会保存下来，之后不会再变；你自己改过开关以后，一律以你的选择为准。Tivon 只保存适用哪一种，不保存你的国家，也不会发送它。

产品分析——帮我们了解 App 的哪些部分真正有用。开启后会发送 Tivon 的使用事件，例如你完成了设置、出现了一次停顿以及你对它的回应，或你查看了订阅页、开始了订阅。视事件不同，可能包含：

  • 停顿：你离开还是继续、做出选择用了多久、所在时段（一天分为五段，例如上午）、显示的停顿形态
  • 设置与自动化：你设置了几个 App、在设置指南里走到了第几步；如果 App 在 Tivon 内置的目录里，还有它的类别（例如社交、视频）。从不包含 App 名称或你输入的文字
  • 购买：Tivon 的订阅产品（月度或年度），从不包含价格或支付信息

每个事件还会附带 App 版本与构建号、iOS 版本、设备型号与类型、Tivon 显示的语言、你的方案（免费版或 Pro）、所在周（例如 2026-W38）、事件发生的准确时间、会话 ID、把相关事件串起来的随机编号（例如每次停顿一个），以及一个随机生成的匿名标识符；另有你看到的同意页或说明页的版本、分析组件的版本等技术信息。事件中不包含任何你设置的 App 的名称或标识，也不包含你在快捷指令里为它输入的文字。

崩溃上报——帮我们修复崩溃。开启后，崩溃发生时会发送崩溃类型、错误信息、各线程的调用栈、已加载的二进制列表，以及基本的 App 与设备信息。同时会发送 App 的打开次数和使用时长，以及一个用于统计这些次数的随机安装 ID，这是计算崩溃率所必需的。截图、视图层级和交互轨迹均已关闭。

两个随机标识符

产品分析使用一个随机生成的匿名标识符，崩溃上报使用另一个随机安装 ID。两者都在你的设备上生成，不含个人信息，也不与你的姓名、邮箱或 Apple 账户关联。关掉分析开关时，两者都会从你的设备上删除。

我们从不收集的内容

  • 你设置的 App 的名称或标识
  • 你的姓名、邮箱或联系方式（如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容）
  • 位置、通讯录、照片、健康数据、浏览或搜索记录
  • 广告标识符（IDFA）或供应商标识符（IDFV），以及任何跨 App、跨网站的追踪

关闭

打开 Tivon 的设置，关掉「分析与崩溃报告」即可。两项服务都会立即停止，设备上尚未发送的数据会被删除；关闭期间发生的事情以后也不会补发。已经开始上传的数据无法撤回。关掉的那一刻，Tivon 还会发送最后一条事件：除了每个事件都附带的基本信息（见上文），只记录开启了多少天；之后不会再发送任何内容。

谁在处理这些数据

产品分析由 PostHog, Inc. 处理，崩溃上报由 Sentry（Functional Software, Inc.）处理，两者的服务器均位于欧盟，并作为受托方按我们的指示处理数据。我们不希望任何一方保留你的 IP 地址：App 会要求 PostHog 不根据 IP 推断位置，并设置 Sentry 不记录 IP；两边的项目都已设置为丢弃 IP 地址。和任何网络请求一样，连接本身仍会从你的 IP 地址到达它们的服务器。

关于购买

订阅完全由 Apple 处理，我们不会接触你的支付信息。如果你开启了产品分析，我们还会收到上文所述的购买事件，其中包含订阅产品（月度或年度）。管理或取消订阅请在“设置”App 中你的 Apple 账户下操作。

关于年龄

Tivon 面向成年人，不以儿童为对象。我们不会有意收集未达到所在国家或地区数字同意年龄的人的数据——部分欧盟国家为 16 岁，其他不少地区为 13 岁。在默认开启的地区，第一页就会说明分析已开启，并且可以当场关掉；关掉之后即停止收集，设备上尚未发送的数据也会被删除。

你的权利

由于上文所述的标识符都是随机的，不与你的姓名、邮箱或账户关联，Tivon 也不会向你显示它们，我们通常无法在收到请求时定位到“你的”数据。你可以随时用上面的开关停止全部收集，卸载 App 即可删除本地保存的全部内容。对本政策有任何疑问，欢迎来信。

政策变更

如果本政策的变更影响到所收集的内容，我们会更新本页和顶部日期，并在必要时重新征求你的同意。
