The selected plan renews monthly or yearly until cancelled. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and the renewal is charged within 24 hours before the period ends.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
